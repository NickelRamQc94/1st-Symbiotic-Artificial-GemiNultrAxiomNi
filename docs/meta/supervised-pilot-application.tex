\section*{Summary}
Request for a 90-day supervised pilot of autofocus smart glasses under qualified ophthalmological oversight. The goal is to evaluate accessibility on an uncommon visual and neurocognitive profile using structured safety thresholds and informed consent.
\section*{Profile}
Bilateral optic disc drusen, mixed astigmatism, +6.00 D reading correction, prior bacterial corneal ulcer/keratitis with photophobia, and contraindication to contact lenses. ADHD Combined and severe time blindness are reported and should be clinically verified.
\section*{Protocol}
\textbf{Weeks 1--2:} Optical calibration, IXI/ViXion evaluation, and SDK integration.\\
\textbf{Weeks 3--12:} Daily logs, bi-weekly reports, and biphasic performance tests.\\
\textbf{Safety:} Halt for worsening photophobia, redness, pain, vision changes, or clinician-defined intraocular-pressure concerns; maintain scheduled examinations.
\section*{Value}
A supervised pilot can produce useful accessibility data while protecting participant safety and preserving informed consent.
